# Output files

`outputs/` holds the reference results. `python reproduce.py` writes a complete run, including the arrays and ledgers that are not stored in the repository, to `reproduction/` (see [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md#reproduction-and-comparison)).

Monetary amounts, returns and statistics are unrounded float64. CSV tables store returns and losses as decimal fractions; displayed percentages multiply by 100, and displayed contrasts are percentage points. Returns are two-year returns and are not annualized.

## Table columns

`scenario_metrics.csv` (keyed by `scenario_id`, `strategy`) contains the mean net return and its interval (`mean_ci_low`, `mean_ci_high`), the standard error of the mean (`mean_mc_se`), ES95 and its interval, the probabilities and counts of a loss (`W < W0`) and of a nonpositive outcome, the count and probability of external funding, the upper bound `3/N` when no funding event occurs, mean and maximum `F/W0`, mean fees, harvest count and frequency, the mean outcome in SEK, and the bootstrap standard deviations with their ratio to the direct standard error.

`contrasts.csv` (keyed by `scenario_id`, `contrast`) contains, for A minus B, the mean difference with its interval and standard error, the ES95 difference `delta_es95 = ES95(A) − ES95(B)` with its interval, the median and 5%/95% quantiles of the paired differences, the probability that A does better, the number of harvests and the mean difference on paths with a harvest, bootstrap standard deviations, and the interval classifications `mean_direction` and `es_direction` (`positive`, `negative` or `numerically_unresolved` when the interval includes zero). A positive mean difference favors A; a negative ES95 difference means lower tail loss for A.

The supplementary tables have the same columns, keyed by `experiment_id` instead of `scenario_id`, plus `experiment_role`, `initial_wealth_sek` and `tax_mode`. `supplementary_contrasts.csv` adds the differences in SEK (`*_sek`, the decimal difference times `W0`). `supplementary_metrics.csv` adds `paths_with_annual_deficit_over_100k`: the number of paths on which a depå strategy has a capital deficit (70% of a net loss) above 100,000 SEK in at least one tax year.

Empty cells mean "not applicable" (for example a conditional mean with no qualifying paths); NaN never occurs. Intervals are NumPy linear 2.5% and 97.5% percentiles of 2,000 paired bootstrap draws: approximate pointwise 95% Monte Carlo intervals conditional on the model.

Load arrays with `numpy.load(path, allow_pickle=False)`:

```python
from pathlib import Path
import numpy as np

root = Path("reproduction/arrays")
with np.load(root / "outcomes/baseline_sweden_lag6__AD_HARVEST.npz") as a:
    r = a["net_return"]                  # shape (20000,), two-year net return
    w = a["W"]                           # bank - F after all settlements
    taxes = a["tax_y1"] + a["tax_y2"]    # signed assessments (positive = owed)
```

In the outcome arrays, missing months are −1. `isk_base_applicable` marks the ISK rows; depå rows hold zeros in the ISK fields. `annual_net_gains` has shape (N, 2), `quarter_values` (N, 2, 4) with a zero first January value, and settled months (N, 2). Every tax record, including a zero tax, settles exactly once. Negative `W` and positive `F` are valid observations and are kept.

Ledger rows are ordered by `month`, `phase_order` and `sequence`. Transaction fields separate gross value, net proceeds or budget, acquisition cost removed, deduction used and fees. Tax records contain assessment, due and settlement months. Quarter-opening values use the price before that month's return.
